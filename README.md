**Game Instructions**



Welcome! You are a magician trapped in a fantasy world. Activate all 4 buttons scattered across the map to unlock the exit gate, then safely escape to win!



**CONTROLS**

\- **Keyboard W/A/S/D**: Move one grid cell at a time. You cannot move beyond map boundaries.

\- **Keyboard E**: Cast a fire spell around you. The spell lasts up to 5 seconds, but disappears immediately if it defeats a Thunderbird.

\- **Keyboard Q**: Once the exit gate (top right corner of the map) is unlocked, stand beneath it and press Q to escape.



**GAMEPLAY**

\- **Buttons**: To unlock the exit, find all 4 buttons across the map. Stand on a button consistently for 2 seconds to activate it.

\- **Thunderbirds**: Thunderbirds spawn randomly and chase you, trying to stop your escape. Each of their attack deals 10 damage.

\- **Fire Spells**: Use your fire spells to defeat approaching Thunderbirds.

\- **Treasure Chests**: Hidden chests that can help you on your journey. Stand on a chest for 1 second to open it and receive either health or additional fire spells. Each chest can only be opened once.

\- Note: There is no cap on fire spells, but your health can only go up to its maximum of 100. This means that if you are at full health and open a health chest, nothing will happen.



**SCORING**

The game displays three numerical values that change based on player actions:

\- **Buttons**: Tracks your progress toward unlocking the exit. Each successfully activated button increases your button score by 1, up to 4.

\- **Health**: Starts at 100. Taking damage from Thunderbirds decreases your health, while opening health chests restores it. Maximum health is 100.

\- **Fire Spells**: Starts at 2. Casting a spell decreases your spell count by 1, while opening spell chests increases it. There is no maximum spell limit.



**WINNING \& LOSING**

\- **Win**: Activate all 4 buttons, then successfully escape through the exit gate.

\- **Lose**: Your health reaches 0.



Good luck on your journey!
